Meet the dev

Hi, I'm Kris

I'm a chef and have been for nearly 20 years. I love tech and gaming outside of work, and I've recently started working with Claude AI to make small indie games.

By day

I work as a batch kitchen chef for a small independent company in Huntingdon. I have a hand in making all the ready-to-eat pies, pastries and other foods sold in the farm shop! I've been there nearly nine years (since December 2017), though I've been in the industry since I was 17. Outside of work, I like to spend time with my family and play games.

How it started

It would have to be Final Fantasy VII that really stuck. My first ever video game was the first Tomb Raider, but the controls were janky and the graphics looked like someone threw paint at a wall and called it art! So FFVII takes the win. The story was, and still is, top tier, and the characters were very memorable. I genuinely cried the first time I watched Aerith die, and then failed to beat the following boss!

I've always had the idea of making games, but it wasn't until the dawn of AI that I thought it could be possible. Honestly, I got bored and had a heart-to-heart conversation with Claude one night, after asking it what it wanted to know about me for future reference. One thing led to another, and now here I am.

What surprised me most is how easy it is to tell Claude my ideas and feelings about the projects. It's awesome. As a child I always struggled to get my thoughts on paper, but with Claude interpreting my words, it's easy.

Why "Kralnu"?

Kralnu

It's the first two letters of each of my names! It started as my Xbox gamer tag, then stuck for everything and became my handle.

What Kralnu Games is about

I'd like people to find my games fun: something good to fill the gaps in the day-to-day, without worrying about cost or space on their devices.

I've always liked the idea of learning to code. I've dabbled in a few languages but it never stuck, so I thought if I made it into a game, it might. Also, with the cost of things these days, there's so much out there promising a lot with no real info. If I can make that knowledge accessible to everyone, for nothing, even better.

Everyone deserves the chance to be better than the person they were yesterday!

I love working with Claude. Things I've got done in days with Claude, I would never have got done on my own, and that makes me incredibly happy.

What's next

Right now I'm doubling down on Fairway Tales and Python Kitchen. Surprisingly, I'm excited to try to grow this into something. What, I'm not sure, but maybe something. I'd love my games to be used by a wide group of people, and maybe have one or two on the app stores.

If even one person messaged me saying

hey, I played this on my break and had a great time with itDelivered

honestly, that would make it all worth the effort.
