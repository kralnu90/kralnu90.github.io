Peekaboo Meadow

A sunny meadow where everything does something. Animals pop out of the bushes, the sun giggles, clouds rain flowers and balloons float up to be popped.

  • A friendly voice asks "Can you find the fox?" with a picture, so no reading is needed
  • Every find earns a star, and five stars starts a balloon party
  • Nothing to get wrong: every tap gets a sound, a surprise or a new word
  • A press-and-hold grown-ups button for sounds, voice and the find-it game
