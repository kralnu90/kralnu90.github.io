Golf RPG

Fairway Tales

Swipe to swing, collect clubs and beat your rivals, from a sleepy country club all the way to the Tour Championship.

  • Five chapters and 45 holes: parkland, windswept links, a desert resort, a snowy mountain and a clifftop course above the clouds
  • A swing that reads your whole stroke, and gets tougher as your handicap drops
  • 53 clubs to level up, plus heads, shafts, grips and your choice of ball
  • Play with friends online: rooms for up to 4, same pins and wind, live scores

Survival fishing adventure

Tight Lines

Read the water, fight every fish the way real anglers do, sell your catch across four towns and get back to the inn before the cold, hunger or the dark gets you.

  • 23 fish across a river, a lake and the coast, each with its own fight, plus crab and lobster pots
  • Motion controls on phones that support them: tilt to steer the rod and flick the phone to cast
  • Hunt, forage and cook: 19 recipes, salting and smoking, and dice-roll events on the road
  • Four towns to trade in, with request boards, reputation, festivals and market gossip
  • Drive between them, and save up for a van or your own campervan
  • Hardcore mode: one life, and your name on the tavern's memory board

Tactical card game

SOLARIS GAMBIT

Nine squares. Ten cards. One winner. Place your cards, beat the numbers on their sides and turn the board your colour.

  • Collect cards from packs and from the rivals you beat
  • House rules like Mirror, Sum, Chain and Aether to mix up every game
  • Four story chapters covering all eight elements, from Fire & Ice to Earth & Wind
  • Daily challenge, puzzles, a gauntlet and a tournament
  • Forge your own signature card and power it up

Forty-four parlour, family, pub, casino and word games in one

The Grand Tabletop Compendium

Pull up a chair in a candlelit parlour. Royal strategy, tumbling dice, pub cards, quiet puzzles, word games, family favourites, a murder to solve and a dungeon to delve, all at one table.

  • Chess, Go, Shogi, Reversi, Four in a Row, Backgammon and more against the AI, or pass and play with a friend on one phone
  • Pub classics Cribbage and Gin Rummy, dice games Yacht and Liar's Dice, plus Battleships and daily Sudoku
  • Family night: Snakes & Ladders, Ludo, Dominoes and Colour Clash, a fast wild-card game for up to six
  • Word games: Tilecraft (crossword tiles), Word Lock (a daily five-letter puzzle), Word Wheel and Word Grid
  • Murder at Marrowgate Manor, an original whodunit, and Torchlight Delve, an original dungeon crawl for a party of heroes
  • Mahjong Solitaire, Klondike and FreeCell for a quiet ten minutes. Every Mahjong deal can be solved
  • Scoundrel and Regicide: roguelike card games where a standard deck becomes a dungeon and a castle full of bosses
  • Easy, Normal and Hard opponents, and a trophy cabinet for every win
