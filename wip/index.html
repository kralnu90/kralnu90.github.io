Time-loop dungeon crawler

30 × to Remember

A wizard's spell went wrong and left Aetherfall stuck in time. You remember every loop. Nobody else does.

  • Every action costs time, and beating enemies wins it back
  • The loop always resets: your levels stay, and only soulbound gear survives
  • Turn-based fights with a party of up to four from eight classes, and a spider-web skill tree for your hero
  • Lose a companion and they might come back as a stranger who doesn't remember you
  • A growing bestiary: 16 creatures, each with its own trick, and Elite, Champion and Dread ranks
