Board game meets action RPG

Crystal Circuit

Race three AI rivals round the board for 30 rounds. Roll the dice, fight in real time when fate calls, and play cards to get ahead.

  • Roll 2d6 to move, and a 12-sided Fate Die decides what each tile holds
  • Real-time fights: move, guard, dodge, use your class skill and cast spells on the move
  • Six classes, and as many heroes as you like
  • Every player has a deck: hostile cards, hidden traps, Ward and Mirror, and Pass the Buck
  • Rivals hold grudges against whoever hurts them
  • Bosses on rounds 10, 20 and 30, and podium prizes for distance and damage

Time-loop dungeon crawler

30 × to Remember

A wizard's spell went wrong and left Aetherfall stuck in time. You remember every loop. Nobody else does.

  • Every action costs time, and beating enemies wins it back
  • The loop always resets: your levels stay, and only soulbound gear survives
  • Turn-based fights with a party of up to four from eight classes, and a spider-web skill tree for your hero
  • Lose a companion and they might come back as a stranger who doesn't remember you, while the creature that killed them keeps their name
  • A growing bestiary: 16 creatures, each with its own trick, and Elite, Champion and Dread ranks
  • A hamlet, dungeon and battles drawn in detailed pixel art, and characters who talk from their own portraits
