About

Kralnu Games is a one-person studio. I make the games I want to play: proper progression, characters with a bit of personality, and controls built for one thumb on a phone.

All seven games are still growing, with new chapters, rivals, waters, tickets and jobs on the way. Feedback shapes what comes next, so every game has a review button in its menu.
