Golf RPG

Fairway Tales

Swipe to swing, collect clubs and beat your rivals, from a sleepy country club all the way to a tour card.

  • Four chapters and 36 holes: parkland, windswept links, a desert resort and a snowy mountain course
  • A swing that reads your whole stroke, and gets tougher as your handicap drops
  • 44 clubs to level up, plus heads, shafts and grips to tune your setup
  • Challenge links: your friends play the same pins and wind on their own phones

Tactical card game

SOLARIS GAMBIT

Nine squares. Ten cards. One winner. Place your cards, beat the numbers on their sides and turn the board your colour.

  • Collect cards from packs and from the rivals you beat
  • House rules like Mirror, Sum, Chain and Aether to mix up every game
  • Daily challenge, puzzles, a gauntlet and a tournament
  • Forge your own signature card and power it up

Survival fishing adventure

Tight Lines

Read the water, fight every fish the way real anglers do, and get back to the inn before the cold, hunger or the dark gets you.

  • 23 fish across a river, a lake and the coast, each with its own way of fighting
  • Float, ledger, spinning and fly fishing, and each one plays differently
  • Survive on a day and night clock: fires, cooking, clean water and a warm bed
  • Sell your catch for Fish Bucks and work up from a willow whip to Master gear
  • Hardcore mode: one life, and your name on the tavern's memory board

About

Kralnu Games is a one-person studio. I make the games I want to play: proper progression, characters with a bit of personality, and controls built for one thumb on a phone.

All three games are still growing, with new chapters, rivals, waters and modes on the way. Feedback shapes what comes next, so every game has a review button in its menu.
